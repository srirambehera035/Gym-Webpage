Nutrition
Indian Diet Plans

Our nutritionists build plans using wholesome Indian foods — high in protein, balanced in carbs, suited to your goal.

Balanced Indian Nutrition Protein · Carbs · Healthy Fats
Why Indian Food Works

You don't need foreign supplements or fancy meal preps. India's traditional kitchen is packed with muscle-building proteins, gut-friendly fibre and anti-inflammatory spices.

🥚

High-Protein Indian Sources

Paneer, moong dal, soyabean, eggs, curd, chicken and fish are all complete, affordable protein sources.

🌾

Smart Carbohydrates

Replace maida with whole wheat roti, brown rice, oats, and sweet potato for sustained energy and better recovery.

💧

Hydration & Timing

Drink 3–4 litres of water daily. Have a protein-rich meal within 45 minutes of finishing your workout.
